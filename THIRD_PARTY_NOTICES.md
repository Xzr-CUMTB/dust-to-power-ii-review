# Data and third-party notices

The original numerical routines and project-trained parameters are supplied for scientific review. No open-source license for the project has been granted. The installation uses independently distributed Python packages under their respective licenses. This archive contains no vendor library source or publisher article. The research and review software was developed with assistance from OpenAI Codex.

The included PV-derived cases use PVOD v1.0, by Tiechui Yao, Jue Wang, Haoyan Wu, Pei Zhang, Shigang Li, Yangang Wang, Xuebin Chi and Min Shi, Science Data Bank. The case files contain selected, transformed power series, model features and simulated dispatch outcomes. They retain attribution under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [PVOD record](https://doi.org/10.11922/sciencedb.01094).

GFS effective-time and source fingerprints identify archived operational forecasts. The original 6-hour radiation and total-cloud fields are obtained through the UCAR GDEX catalog. Model inputs in the example are processed features. [UCAR GDEX](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d084001/).

The observational air source is the CNEMC-derived historical station archive. Monitor metadata is from Zenodo. Source fingerprints and programmatic retrieval logic are included, while the national station observations and metadata spreadsheets are obtained from the original providers. [Historical air archive](https://quotsoft.net/air/) · [Monitor metadata](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7152703).

The tabulated Hong Kong scores are derived from the data of Zinan Lin, Qi Zhou, Zhe Wang, Ce Wang, Davis Boyd Bookhart and Marcus Leung-Shea. The original archive is CC0. [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.m37pvmd99).

Utrecht point-power score summaries derive from the 175-system dataset *Open-source quality control routine and multi-year power generation data of 175 PV systems*. The unfiltered source file has published MD5 0bd1947e87e4f9b6302c3c82d2217a0d. [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10953360).
